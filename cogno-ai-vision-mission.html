How it works

Three steps, one smart robot

The same path real AI engineers follow: gather good data, train and test a model, then put it to work in the field.

01

Collect data

Name each object and snap photos from the laptop camera or the robot's phone camera.

  • Rotate, flip, mirror, switch camera
  • Hold to shoot a burst of photos
  • Saved on your device only
02

Train & send

The AI learns every object, tests itself on photos it never saw, then you send the model to the robot camera.

  • Accuracy score and mix-ups
  • Live test on the camera
  • One tap to send to the robot
03

Mission

Drive with the keyboard to find the START QR, memorise the rules, capture targets in order and finish at END.

  • W A S D driving over Bluetooth
  • The AI labels every capture
  • Instant score and upload

Scoring

Same kit. Different skill.

Every team has identical hardware, so points reward training quality, driving and memory.

Better data, better AI

Varied photos (angles, light, distance) make the AI sure of itself in the arena.

Memory under pressure

The mission hides after a few seconds. Peeking costs points.

Smooth driving

Speed keys, trim and slow mode reward careful control; collisions cost points.

No faking

The AI, not the team, names every photo. Wrong captures lose points.

FAQ

Good questions

Do the phone and laptop need the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. The video goes straight across your local Wi-Fi. The internet is only used to load the app and to introduce the two devices the first time.

Which browser should we use?

Chrome or Edge on the laptop (Bluetooth needs it). Any modern browser on the phone. The page must be opened from an https:// link so the phone camera is allowed.

How many photos per object?

At least 10, ideally 30 or more from different angles, distances and light. Also add photos of the empty track to "Nothing / floor" so the robot knows what is not an object.

Where is our data stored?

Only in this browser on your laptop. The trash button in the top bar erases it all when you are done.

Who creates the missions?

Organisers sign in to Admin, build missions, and print START, END and checkpoint QR cards to place on the track.
